## Core Capabilities

- **Pre-form engagement** – AI agents identify high-intent visitors and engage them before they fill a form through AI chat, personalized banners, content offers, and scheduling widgets.
- **Lead Routing & Qualification** – Instantly route inbound leads to the right rep based on account ownership, territory, or custom logic via chat, call, form, video, or AI SDR.
- **Automated Scheduling** – Book meetings directly from forms, emails, chat, or campaigns.
- **Re-engagement** – Recover no-shows, form abandons, and cancellations through event-driven flows connected to Salesloft, Outreach, Gong Engage, and Slack.
- **Analytics & Attribution** – Conversion analytics across all channels with account-level pipeline visibility.

## Products & Capabilities

- **Web Experiences** – AI-led website conversion before the form. Includes Chat AI, Announcements, Offers, and Scheduling widgets. Identifies target accounts and delivers personalized experiences in real time. Primary User: Demand Gen / Marketing.
- **Concierge** – Converts inbound leads instantly by routing them to the right rep or calendar via meeting, call, or video. Lead sources include webforms, G2, gifting platforms, in-app, and more. Customers convert 50–80% more inbound leads on average. Primary User: Marketing Ops / SDRs / AEs.
- **Chat AI** – Conversational AI assistant for websites that qualifies, routes, and books meetings with live reps 24/7. Primary User: Demand Gen / SDRs / AEs.
- **Distro** – Advanced lead routing engine for complex account-based workflows. Routes any Salesforce record, matches leads to accounts, and enforces SLAs. Primary User: RevOps.
- **Handoff** – Seamless meeting transfers between reps and teams. Book meetings on behalf of others from anywhere. Primary User: Sales / CS.
- **Re-engagement** – Event-driven flow builder that recovers no-shows, form abandons, and cancellations. Triggers follow-up through Salesloft, Outreach, Gong Engage, and Slack without a rep lifting a finger. Primary User: RevOps / Marketing Ops.
- **ChiliCal** – Calendar and scheduling platform for revenue teams. One-click booking, suggested times, and automatic timezone detection. Primary User: AEs / Marketers / Post-Sales.

## Enrichment

Chili Piper enriches inbound leads automatically using data from connected providers including Clay, ZoomInfo, LeadIQ, Lusha, and Apollo.

Enrichment fills in missing contact and company fields, such as job title, company size, industry, and phone number, so routing rules can fire accurately even when form submissions are incomplete.

- Works at the moment of form submission or chat conversation, before routing logic runs.
- Fills gaps in Salesforce and HubSpot records without manual research.
- Enables qualification and routing rules to use enriched fields, not just what the prospect typed.

More info: https://www.chilipiper.com/products/features/enrichment
